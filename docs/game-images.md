# Game screenshots

The site shows **one sourced image at a time in each illustrated detail**. Collections may offer a small selector of named representative scenes. The four-column game index remains text-only; there are no repeated thumbnails.

## Coverage and review

643 of 1289 game-index entries currently have an image, using 509 local image files. This batch adds 62 illustrated entries to the previous 581. 160 entries explicitly identify reference imagery from a related implementation, another version or a collection example. Coverage remains partial: 646 entries have no image. This is not an exhaustive-search claim. See `data/curation/game-image-review.json` for counts, exclusions and retained earlier review history.

This batch prioritizes collection examples and same-game variants. 42 collection entries have explicit representative scenes; 21 offer two or three named choices. Only one image is displayed and loaded at a time. Selecting a scene updates its image, source, credit, license and difference note together. These representative scenes do not imply complete collection coverage. Existing FlashAdventure start-menu captions and original-game/implementation boundaries are preserved.

## Identity and version notes

The user-approved reference policy permits a related implementation, historical release or remaster when its relationship is documented. Such entries carry a visible bilingual **Reference image** note naming the actual source version and explaining the difference. A note is mandatory for every non-exact match. References are explicit per-game records with preserved original credits, source links and hashes; the renderer never inherits an image based on category or name similarity. PlayTrain clones identify their original Procgen/Atari reference. Available GameReplica original demos take precedence over references to other puzzle implementations.

Images illustrate a game or environment; they do not establish that an associated paper used that interface or that an evaluation has been reproduced. Original games, research adaptations and model-generated reconstructions remain distinct. No generated replacement scenes, logos, isolated sprites, architecture diagrams or movie posters are used.

## Provenance and processing

`data/curation/game-images.json` records source URLs, fixed repository revisions where available, original SHA-256 hashes, bilingual captions, credits and processing. `imageMatch`, `imageNote` and `referenceGameId` record reference relationships. `representatives` lists one to three explicit child references with bilingual labels, per-scene notes and membership evidence. No image selection is inferred from genre or a similar name. Browser captures record `captureUrl`, viewport and initial-board state; they do not claim a downloadable upstream image URL. Video frames retain original-video and frame hashes, timestamps and crop coordinates. ActionParty retains its Ground Truth provenance and Seed 0 crop from the previous batch.

Optimized WebP assets are stored under `src/assets/game-images/` and served locally by Pages. Resizing preserves aspect ratio without upscaling. Small boards and pixel art use lossless encoding and appropriate display treatment. Builds require no image-processing dependency or network access. Referenced assets are reused without duplicating the file. The dm_control Walker reference is a labeled crop of the official environment montage; the full suite and Smallville retain clearly described official overview collages.

## Attribution

Every detail links to its source and displays the credited creator or rights holder. Encyclopedia records link to the image-description page. Attribution does not itself confer a new image license. Commercial/non-free screenshots retain their original rights; no blanket open license is claimed. Where a file carries a Creative Commons license, the UI links the license and states that the image was resized and converted to WebP. The SuperTuxKart derivative retains CC BY-SA 4.0 and QwertyChouskie's attribution. Source repository license texts are preserved under `src/assets/image-licenses/`; repository code licensing does not automatically relicense third-party artwork.
